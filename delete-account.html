Delete My Account

You can request the deletion of your Profinance AI account and all associated data at any time. We process every deletion request within 30 days of receipt.

How to Request Deletion

Choose either method below:

  1. From inside the app
    Open Profinance AI → tap your Profile / Settings → scroll to Account → tap Delete Account and confirm. Your data is removed immediately.
  2. By email
    Send a request to team@intellminds.com with the subject line "Delete Account – Profinance AI" and include the email address associated with your account. We will confirm deletion within 30 days.

What Gets Deleted

Data type Action
Account & profile information Deleted
All expense history Deleted
Income records Deleted
Streak & badge data Deleted
App settings & preferences Deleted

Data Retention

We do not retain any personal data after account deletion. All data listed above is permanently removed within 30 days of your request. No data is kept for any purpose after that period.

Questions?

If you have any questions about account deletion or your data, contact us at team@intellminds.com.
